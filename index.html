Reinforcement learning · IoT · In progress

Hydroponics AI

2026 · STEAM research grant · DFRobot hardware sponsorship

Problem
Hydroponic farms dose nutrients on fixed thresholds, which wastes nutrients and caps yield.
What I built
Six sensing and control workflows on two ESP32 boards, a FastAPI service, and an RL policy (PyTorch, Gymnasium) that decides dosing, with live video over LiveKit.
Result
Funded by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology’s STEAM grant. DFRobot sponsored the sensors after I pitched the project by email.

PyTorch · Gymnasium · ESP32 (C++) · Raspberry Pi · MQTT · FastAPI · WebSockets · LiveKit · Flutter
